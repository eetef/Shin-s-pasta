Our story

From another world's kitchen

Shin's Pasta opened its doors near the Capsule Corp side of West City, just past the Meta House. What started as a modest noodle stand has become the go-to spot for warriors, gods, and civilians alike who need a real meal between training arcs. Every dish is made fresh. No shortcuts. No energy blasts in the kitchen.

S

Shin

Shin was once the weakest of the five Supreme Kais. He was also the only one who survived Majin Buu's rampage while the other four were killed or absorbed. He tried to stop Buu himself and couldn't. He needed Goku and the others to finish what he started. After the dust settled, Shin realized guarding the universe wasn't a job he could carry alone or take credit for. He stepped back from being a symbol of power he never really had. He poured that same effort into something he could actually be great at. Shin's Pasta is his attempt to build something real, one plate at a time, on his way to making it the best restaurant in Universe 7.

K

Kibito

Long before he was known simply as Shin's attendant, Kibito was a capable Supreme Kai aide in his own right. He was skilled in Kai Kai teleportation and healing techniques few others ever mastered. When the other Supreme Kais were killed by Majin Buu, Kibito lost the wider circle he once served. He became Shin's sole bodyguard and closest companion by necessity, not just title. He carried that loyalty into the kitchen. He may not draw the same attention as the CEO. But every dish that leaves this kitchen has his exact, healer's precision behind it.
